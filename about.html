About The Project

Why JobReady SG exists

JobReady SG is a C240 AI-in-Action project — a multi-agent AI career coach built for Republic Polytechnic students, School of Infocomm. It exists to close the gap between graduating and feeling ready to job-hunt, by giving every student round-the-clock access to resume feedback, interview practice, and Singapore-specific industry insight.
