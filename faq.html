FAQ

Good questions

Does this replace career coaches?

No — it complements them. JobReady SG handles the repetitive first pass (resume checks, practice questions) so your time with a human career coach goes further.

How does the AI generate recommendations?

A Supervisor Agent routes your message to the right specialist, which answers using Google Gemini grounded on a NotebookLM knowledge base built from RP and MOM career resources.

Is my information stored?

There's no live database of student conversations. The knowledge the agents draw on — RP and MOM career resources — was summarised once with NotebookLM into a fixed reference used by the Industry Tips Agent's prompt; your chat messages are only used to generate that session's reply.

Who is this designed for?

Republic Polytechnic students preparing for internships or their first full-time role.
